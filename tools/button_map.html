SAM3D · console and cabin data as built

Button Map

Every control in the console, traced to what it switches on, what that feeds, and which lamp, display or launch it ends in. Drawn by tools/button_map.py from the console and cabin files and the engagement model's sensor mapping. Work that isn't built yet is marked in red.

Signal flow

Read left to right. Click any box to trace its chain upstream and downstream; click empty space to clear.

    drag to pan · wheel or pinch to zoom

    Crew drill

    The procedures the NPC crew and the tutor follow, one seat each. Steps are in order; amber lines are what a step waits for. Click a control to find it on the map.

    Missing and incomplete

    Placed in the cabin but not wired, or not built at all. Phases refer to docs/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md.

    Control index

    All controls with their seat, panel, start position and state.

    States: wired drives a subsystem or the launch; model is read by the engagement model (radars, IFF, TV, wheels, orientation); indicator only lights a lamp or turns on a display; inert is placed in the cabin with no function yet. Guards with no own entry in the cabin file are drawn over the control they cover.
