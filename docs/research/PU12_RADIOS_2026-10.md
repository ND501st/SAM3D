# The PU-12M's R-111 and R-407: research (October 2026)

Why: the PU-12M had both sets as a power switch and a warm-up (`docs/research/PU12_RESEARCH_2026-10.md` open question 2).
This note settles what each set is, every control on its front, how it is worked, and what of that matters to the post,
from the sets' own documents. Read 19 October 2026. Where the sim uses a number, `data/systems/pu12m_radios.json` cites it.

## The R-111 (FuG-3: the regiment's command net)

**What it is** (`r111_to` p. 5): a vehicle VHF FM set for search-free communication, retuned automatically, standing
or on the move, to one of four frequencies prepared in advance (ЗПЧ). It works with telecode equipment, is remote
controlled from consoles or a field telephone, and (the double version) runs two transceivers on one antenna and relays
automatically or by hand.

**Data** (`r111_to` pp. 6-8):

| | |
|---|---|
| Band | 20.0-52.0 MHz, two sub-bands 20-36 and 36-52 MHz; 1281 channels 25 kHz apart |
| Presets | 4 (ЗПЧ), mechanical; preparing all four takes 4 min |
| Retuning | at most 45 s overall (the preset mechanism, then the power amplifier and the antenna tuner tune themselves) |
| Frequency | to ±6 kHz on the scale 5 min after switching on; ±2 kHz on the quartz calibrator; a preset comes back to ±1.5 kHz |
| Output | at least 65 W (20-22 MHz), 75 W (22-28 and 50-52), 80 W (28-50) into 75 Ω, hand-tuned; switchable 1 %, 20 %, 100 % |
| Receive to transmit | ≤ 0.7 s |
| Supply | 26 V ±3.9 V; at most 20 A transmitting, 7 A receiving, 2 A on duty reception; above 30 V it switches itself off |
| Duty | 48 h at 3:1 receive to transmit (15 min / 5 min); at most 1 h on end transmitting (4 h with fans) |
| Mass | the simplex set 100 kg without supplies and antenna |

**Ranges** (simplex, to a set of the same type, medium country, any time, a free frequency; `r111_to` pp. 6-7):

| Ends | Range |
|---|---|
| Both on the move, the 3.4 m whip on a screened vehicle at up to 40 km/h | ≥ 35 km |
| One standing on a whip with counterpoises on an 11 m mast, the other moving on its whip | ≥ 45 km |
| Both on whips with counterpoises on 11 m masts | ≥ 60 km |

`flak11` gives the PU-12's R-111 25-30 km (the NVA handbook, conditions not stated). The model takes the ТО's table and
the antennas each end has up; the NVA figure is recorded beside it.

**The antenna tuner** (САУ, `r111_to` pp. 10-11) tunes for three antennas: the 3.4 m whip in the middle of the roof, the
whip with three counterpoises on an 11 m mast, a broadband antenna on an 11-18 m mast. Its antenna distributor is set
to ШТЫРЬ (whip) or ТЕЛЕСКОП (mast) before work (p. 64). The PU-12M's 16 m mast is in the third case. The ČSLA plan puts
the SAU block (item 26) in the commander's corner, under the bench's back.

**Order of work** (`r111_to` pp. 63-70, the warnings pp. 3-4):

1. РЕЖ. РАБОТЫ to ОТКЛ., the vehicle's supply on; ИНДИКАТОР to БОРТ. СЕТЬ: the needle in its sector. ВИД РАБОТЫ to ТЛФ, the
   headset plugged in, the antenna distributor to ШТЫРЬ or ТЕЛЕСКОП.
2. The frequency: a preset button (the mechanism turns to it and stops), or ПЛАВНО and the scale by the
   УСТАНОВКА ЧАСТОТЫ knob, the sub-band switch to 20-36 or 36-52.
3. РЕЖ. РАБОТЫ to МОЩНОСТЬ 20 %, **wait 1.5-3 min** for the output valves' cathodes, press НАСТРОЙКА УМ: the УМ lamp
   lights; when it and the tuner's lamps go out, the set is tuned for receive and transmit. Then РЕЖ. РАБОТЫ to 1, 20
   or 100 % for the distance. At the limit of range tune again by hand for the highest antenna current.
4. Duty reception: РЕЖ. РАБОТЫ ДЕЖ. ПР. (2 A from the supply, the most economical); the headset hisses until a
   correspondent transmits. Telephony (ТЛФ): the power for the distance, press the headset's switch to talk. ДИСТ. УПР.:
   worked from a field telephone on the line terminals up to 500 m away. ТЛК: telecode equipment on the line input.
5. Warnings: switching to another preset on the double set, retune the tuner; with no automatic tuning, ОТКЛ. then 20 %
   and tune again; tune automatically only with the hand-tuning hatch closed; **another set's frequency within 10 %**
   (two sets in one vehicle, or sets nearer than 300 m), and not 8 or 16 MHz apart; ЗАЩИТА ВХОДА lights when a strong
   set near by trips the receiver's input protection: on ДЕЖ. ПР. or 1 % switch the set off and on; on 20 or 100 %
   transmit briefly (press the switch or ВЫЗОВ).

## The R-407 (FuG-5: the regiment's telecode)

**What it is** (`r407_to`, `r407_refs`): a one-channel duplex VHF FM radio-relay set "Дятел", carried in a pack or in a
vehicle, for links between signal vehicles, a channel taken off relay lines, a radio stretch in a wire line. Its
telephone channel's input and output take tone-telegraph and special equipment: in the PU-12M, the ASPD-U's telecode
(`flak11`: the R-407 net of the regiment's automated fire control, to 15 km).

**Data** (`r407_to` via radionic.ru, `r407_plate`):

| | |
|---|---|
| Band | 52-60 MHz, 161 channels 50 kHz apart, set separately for the receiver and the transmitter (two drums, ×100 kHz) |
| Modes | duplex with the receiver 4 MHz from the transmitter; simplex with that spacing; simplex on one frequency |
| Receiver | double superhet, АПЧ (holds a detuning of up to 15 kHz to within 3 kHz); 5 µV; frequency to 8 kHz 15 min after switching on |
| Transmitter | ≥ 1 W into 75 Ω |
| Supply | two 2НКН24 batteries, 4.8 V; 3 A duplex, 3.2 A transmitting simplex, 1.7 A duty reception |
| Ranges | the 1.3 m whip on the set ≥ 3 km; with counterpoises on the 5 m support up to 10 km; on an 11 m mast up to 20 km; on a lowered mast between moving vehicles up to 4 km |
| Size | 250 x 420 x 510 mm; 27 kg in its pack with batteries |

**The front** (`r407_photos`, labels read at 1920 px; the plate's sketch names the main switches):

| Control | Positions |
|---|---|
| ПИТАНИЕ | ВЫКЛ. / on |
| АПЧ | ВЫКЛ. / АПЧ |
| РОД РАБОТЫ (the plate's name) | ДУПЛЕКС, ДЕЖ. ПРИЕМ – СИМПЛЕКС, ТА |
| УСТАНОВКА ЧАСТОТЫ x 100 КГЦ | two drum scales, ПРИЕМНИК (left) and ПЕРЕДАТЧИК (right), each with its knob and a СТОПОР lever |
| КОРР. ПРМ, КОРР. ПРД | correction plugs |
| КАЛИБРАТОР – СВЕТ | push-button (the calibrator, and the scales' light in the dark) |
| УРОВНИ | ПРИЕМ, ИЗМЕРИТ., ПЕРЕДАЧА knobs |
| ПУ (the handset's switch) | РАДИО, КОНТРОЛЬ, ПРОВОД |
| УЗКАЯ / ШИРОКАЯ | toggle |
| ВЫЗОВ | push-button |
| the meter switch | ИЗМЕРИТ., ПЕРЕДАЧА, ПРИЕМ, НАКАЛ, ТОК АНТ. |
| ИЗМЕРИТ. УРОВЕНЬ | push-button |
| the modes switch | 2-ПР. ОКОНЕЧН., (2-ПР.) ТРАНЗИТ, (4-ПР.) ИВ., 4-ПР. ТВ |
| lamps | РАДИО (green), ПРОВОД (red): a call |
| terminals | 2-ПР., 4-ПР. ПРИЕМ, 4-ПР. ПЕРЕДАЧА, КОРПУС |

**Order of work** (`r407_plate`):

* Duty reception and simplex: the handset on; ПУ to РАДИО, the modes switch to 2-ПР. ОКОНЕЧН.; РОД РАБОТЫ to ДЕЖ. ПРИЕМ
  – СИМПЛЕКС; АПЧ on; ПИТАНИЕ on; the receiver's and transmitter's frequencies set with the УСТАНОВКА ЧАСТОТЫ knobs and
  fixed with the СТОПОР levers (in the dark, КАЛИБРАТОР – СВЕТ lights them); УЗКАЯ. A call: a high tone, the green
  РАДИО lamp, the bell. To call, ВЫЗОВ with the handset's switch pressed. **With strong interference, АПЧ off.** The
  receiver's 53.8, 56.85 and 59.5 MHz are not to be assigned.
* Duplex: as above, РОД РАБОТЫ to ДУПЛЕКС; talk with the switch pressed, no need to release it. The transmitter's
  52.5, 55.55 and 58.2 MHz are not to be assigned in duplex. **The receiver must be 4 MHz from the transmitter.**
  Forbidden: retuning the transmitter across the band in duplex; opening the correction plugs without need.
* A 2-wire line: ПУ to ПРОВОД, 2-ПР. ОКОНЕЧН.; a call from the line lights the red ПРОВОД lamp.
* Relay: two sets at least 15 m apart, 4-wire terminals cross-connected, ПУ to КОНТРОЛЬ, the modes switch to 4-ПР. ТВ;
  the levels set on the meter's red mark (ИЗМЕРИТ., ПЕРЕДАЧА, ПРИЕМ).
* A channel to relay stations or other equipment: the channel on the 2-ПР. or 4-ПР. terminals, the modes switch to
  2-ПР. ТРАНЗИТ or 4-ПР. НЧ (the plate's word; the switch reads ИВ.), ПУ to КОНТРОЛЬ to listen.

## Decisions for SAM3D

1. **Both sets are modelled as sets** (`simcore/radio_sets.hpp`), their controls as on the front (the ids keep
   `radio.fug3.*` and `radio.fug5.*`). What is not on the PU-12M's work (the R-111's relay and service modes, the R-407's
   2-wire line and relay) is on the panel and does what the set does: the post is not on the net.
2. **The R-111** is on the regiment's command net when: РЕЖ. РАБОТЫ at ДЕЖ. ПР. or above and the set 5 min warm; on the
   net's frequency (a preset or the scale, the right sub-band) to within half a channel; ВИД РАБОТЫ ТЛФ or ДИСТ. УПР. (ПРМ
   or ОЖИД. hear only), ТЛК off; the regiment within reach. It **transmits** only tuned (НАСТРОЙКА УМ at 20 % after the
   cathodes' 3 min), at the power the switch gives; its reach scales with the power as the fourth root (plane-earth
   propagation: received field ∝ √P h₁h₂ / d², textbook). A preset change runs the mechanism and retunes itself, at most
   45 s: SAM3D splits it 15 s turning, 30 s tuning (the split is an estimate). Ranges: the ТО's table, by which antenna
   each end has up (the post's distributor ШТЫРЬ, or ТЕЛЕСКОП with the mast up; the regiment's mast when it stands
   still). ЗАЩИТА ВХОДА trips when one of the post's own transmitters is within 10 % (or 8 or 16 MHz) of the R-111's
   frequency, and is reset as the ТО says.
3. **The R-407** brings the telecode to the ASPD-U when: ПИТАНИЕ on and the set warm; РОД РАБОТЫ ДУПЛЕКС or ДЕЖ. ПРИЕМ –
   СИМПЛЕКС; the receiver on the regiment's transmitting channel; the modes switch on a 4-wire position (the ASPD-U's
   receiver on the 4-wire channel, SAM3D's reading of the ТО's "special equipment" input, estimate); in duplex the
   transmitter 4 MHz from the receiver and off the three spurious channels; the receiver off its three; within reach
   (mast up 15 km, `flak11`; mast down 4 km, `r407_to`'s lowered-mast figure). With АПЧ on, the set takes the regiment's
   channel even if its drum is a little off; in **interference** (a scenario outage of kind `interference`) АПЧ locks on
   to the noise and the link is lost unless АПЧ is off, as the plate says. Without АПЧ, the drum must be within the
   receiver's narrow band and 15 min warm.
4. **Meters**: both sets' needles are worked out from the console (the R-111's ИНДИКАТОР positions, the R-407's meter
   switch), so every player's machine reads the same.
5. **Where**: the R-111 at the radio operator (plan item 41) with its antenna distributor on the SAU block (item 26); the
   R-407 behind the battery commander (item 25), on a bracket at the side over the BP-3: the plan gives its footprint
   (smaller than the set: the set keeps the plan's front and forward edge), the battery commander's workplace drawing
   its height (item 25 upright behind his seat back, its upper half above it; a first reading stood it on the floor,
   taking the unnumbered box at the drawing's lower right for it, and from his seat its face was hidden behind the
   seat back). The SAU's antenna distributor on the block's inboard end (SAM3D: the plan shows the block from above
   only). The front of each is traced (`tools/traces/r111_front.json`,
   `tools/blender/r111_trace.py`; `r407_front.json`, `r407_trace.py`).
