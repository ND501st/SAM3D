# Terrain picks: free models and textures, checked twice (3 Nov 2026)

Every free model and texture the terrain research picked (`docs/scenery/TERRAIN_ROADMAP.md` sections 4 and 5), with
the second reader's verdict: each page opened again on 3 Nov 2026 to check that it is the asset named, that it is
free and that its licence is the one written. Written from the research workflow's results; the reasons for each
pick, its fit and its concerns are in the notes it came from (`TERRAIN_VEGETATION_2026-11.md`,
`TERRAIN_BUILT_2026-11.md`, `TERRAIN_TEXTURES_2026-11.md`).

215 picks: 205 confirmed, 9 uncertain, 1 refuted. Fab's free listings rotate: check the price and the licence again on the day an asset is claimed,
and register it (`data/scenery/assets.json`) before it is used. Fab-Standard files are never redistributed: they
stay under `unreal/SAM3D/Content/ThirdParty/`. A CC-BY asset is credited.
