# Osa (9A33B family) interior photos on the web: SŁ-2M rows and other panels

Search date: 12 Nov 2026. Searched in Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech, German and English: Commons, valka.cz/armedconflicts,
RWD-MB III (NVA veterans' site), cybermodeler (Duxford), net-maquettes, warwheels, Prime Portal, kitmaker/armorama,
defence24, ArmyInform, Izvestia, AiF/MoD RF, RG, kchf.ru, vpvo.narod.ru, pvo.guns.ru, weaponsystems.net, the Aviationist.
Every image rated medium or high below was downloaded to `scratchpad/review/web/` and looked at (contact sheets `*_sheet.png`).

**Summary: no new straight-on, high-resolution, uncropped view of the whole SŁ-2M was found.** The best new find is
Prasał's oblique 1075 px view, which shows the **whole top row legibly** (0062 with its two meters, the eight yellow
dials of 0094, the four meters of 0004), but it stops above the bottom row. The bottom row (0051-7М, ОК81-4М, ОК51-6М) is
shown whole only in the small (300 px) RWD-MB III photos and, across the left and middle columns, in the composite
the project already has. The book photo already in hand probably comes from Wings & Wheels G061 (row 9 below).

## Notes for the model

- RWD-MB III names the 9A33BM3's rack **SL-2M2** (not SŁ-2M) and the cabinet **OO96-2M2**, and lists SP-1M1, SL-1M1,
  SP-2M2 and SL-3M. Check these against the Osa-AK (9A33BM2) designations in our data. The suffixes may be BM3-specific.
- None of the sources found is a confirmed 9A33BM2 with an unmodified console except possibly row 4/5's unknown vehicle.
  The only confirmed Osa-AK (Armenian) is modernised.
