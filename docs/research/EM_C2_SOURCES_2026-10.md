'
Why: the first E4 design had one source, the battery's PU-12M. Armies that operated the Osa used other feeds as well, and a'

## Model decisions'
'
* A source is an air-picture source (in code a `PlottingBoard`: the marks the source holds, not a board the crew sees;'
  a launcher's crew knows only what it hears, `docs/research/PLOTTING_BOARD_2026-10.md`) with its own coverage (relayed / own radar to the 4.12 (√h₁ + √h₂) km horizon / by eye), cadence,'
  fixed navigation error plus fresh error per plot, voice timing and link range.'
* One voice net carries one message at a time: sources on the same net delay one another. Only the active source is heard.'
* The crew listens to one source. The Osa cabin has two R-123M sets (`mon_2112_80` Fig. 3), the basis for keeping a second net'
  on watch: the commander can tell, without losing his picture, that another source is alive. The model lets him know the state'
  of every listed source, which is optimistic when more than two are configured.'
* Contact = a delivered transmission from the source. 2.5 / 5 reporting periods without contact = degraded / silent (the EM'
  layer's thresholds). An NPC commander falls back down his list when the active source is silent, and goes back to the main'
  source once it has been up for `primary_stable_s` (60 s). A player commander turns `auto_fallback` off and uses'
  `RequestSource`.'
* Changing takes `switch_s` (6 s, an estimate): nothing is heard meanwhile; what the other source said meanwhile is lost.'
* Links: outages (scripted or `c2_fail`), link range from the vehicle, and a `C2LinkModel` (the EM link budget plugs in here).'
'
## Voice designations as EM messages (done 11 Oct 2026)'
'
For a source with an EM radio, each designation is an `em::Message` of the new kind `Voice` (`em::SendVoice`): the board works out when the operator starts and stops reading (shared voice net, 4 s delay, 8 s message); the message goes on the air for exactly those times, so it radiates (ESM can hear and DF it), and at its end the EM layer's delivery law decides, with the jammers and co-site interference of that moment, whether the vehicle got it (`EmSensorLink::SendVoice`, `VoiceOutcome`). A lost message is never heard; the commander's fallback sees the silence. Test: a barrage jammer switched on at 100 s makes the later designations fail. Sources without an EM radio keep the earlier draw.'
'
Not done: a transmission cancelled by an outage still radiates in the EM scene.'
'
## The two R-123M sets (12 Oct 2026)'
'
Source picking now goes through the vehicle's radios, not an abstract switch. The 9A33BM2 has two R-123M sets (`mon_2112_80` p26), each with four fixed presets (`r123_refs`); they share one frequency plan, so a preset names a net and so a source (`c2.presets`, up to four; default: the main source, the fallback list, then the rest). The crew listens to one set, the other is the standby and is monitored. Changes:'
'
* a source on a preset: the standby set is turned to it (or the headset moves to the set already on it): `select_s`, 3 s, an estimate; the old source is heard until then;'
* a source on no preset: the standby set is re-tuned onto it, `retune_s`, 30 s, an estimate;'
* the commander only knows whether the sources on the two sets are alive; if the listened-to one goes silent and the other is silent too, he turns the standby set to the next on his list (a probe), then moves to it if it answers. This replaces the earlier assumption that he knows every source's state;'
* by hand: `TuneSet`, `ListenToSet`, `RequestSource`; Unreal: `TuneC2Radio`, `ListenToC2Radio`, `GetC2RadioPreset`, `GetC2PresetSource` (unbuilt).'
'
Cabin controls (done 12 Oct 2026): `radio.left.preset` (driver's side), `radio.right.preset` and `radio.headset` (commander) in `data/procedures/osa_ak.json`, placed on two new panels on the fronts of the R-123M meshes (`radio_left_panel`, `radio_right_panel`, `data/cabins/osa_ak.json`). The runner keeps them in step with the network (`SyncRadios`): a control the crew moves tunes the set or moves the headset after `select_s`; a change the commander makes moves the control. Positions of the panels are estimates (the meshes' fronts are generic). The sets themselves (power, warm-up, squelch, volume, antenna) and the R-124 are modelled in `simcore/crew_comms.hpp`, see `docs/research/OSA_COMMS_2026-10.md`; NPC crew switch the sets on with `radio_left_on` and `radio_right_on`. Not done: radio audio.'
